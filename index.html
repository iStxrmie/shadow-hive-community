FILE 001 / BIOLOGICAL ARCHIVES

THE VIRUS INDEX.

A field guide to notable Resident Evil pathogens, parasites and related bioweapons — alongside clearly labelled original Shadow Hive fan concepts.

11 FILES AVAILABLE

FILE VX-001 // CANONHIGH THREAT

T-VIRUS

VIRAL AGENT · UMBRELLA RESEARCH

A mutagenic virus developed from the Progenitor virus research programme. Its effects vary by host and exposure, and it is associated with the Raccoon City outbreak.

OPEN RESEARCH FILE
ORIGIN
Umbrella Corporation research, building on Progenitor virus discoveries.
KNOWN EFFECTS
Severe cellular mutation, aggression and reanimation in some infected subjects; outcomes are highly variable.
NOTABLE ASSOCIATIONS
Raccoon City incident, Tyrant programme and numerous infected creatures.
ARCHIVE NOTE
“T-Virus” is a broad label in the series; strains and outcomes differ across incidents.
FILE VX-002 // CANONCRITICAL THREAT

G-VIRUS

VIRAL AGENT · GOLGOTHA PROJECT

A highly unstable mutagenic agent associated with William Birkin and the G-Project. Infection can trigger repeated, extreme physical mutations.

OPEN RESEARCH FILE
ORIGIN
Developed through William Birkin’s research, separate from the T-Virus programme.
KNOWN EFFECTS
Rapid mutation, abnormal growth and unpredictable transformation.
NOTABLE ASSOCIATIONS
William Birkin and the Raccoon City laboratory incident.
ARCHIVE NOTE
G-infection outcomes are especially unpredictable; do not treat every mutation as identical.
FILE VX-003 // CANONSEVERE THREAT

T-VERONICA

VIRAL AGENT · ASHFORD RESEARCH

A virus developed by Alexia Ashford using Progenitor-related research and an unusual fusion with an ant queen gene sequence.

OPEN RESEARCH FILE
ORIGIN
Alexia Ashford’s research, conducted in the Ashford family’s Antarctic and Rockfort Island facilities.
KNOWN EFFECTS
Major physiological changes and altered behaviour; Alexia’s own adaptation involved a prolonged cryogenic process.
NOTABLE ASSOCIATIONS
Alexia Ashford and the events of Resident Evil CODE:Veronica.
ARCHIVE NOTE
Its development and effects are distinct from the T- and G-Virus programmes.
FILE VX-004 // CANONHIGH THREAT

LAS PLAGAS

PARASITIC ORGANISM · SPAIN

An ancient parasitic organism found beneath a rural Spanish region. In modern events, it is used to control and alter hosts by the Los Illuminados cult.

OPEN RESEARCH FILE
ORIGIN
Ancient parasite discovered in underground ruins in Spain.
KNOWN EFFECTS
Neurological influence, host control and physical changes that vary by Plaga type and host.
NOTABLE ASSOCIATIONS
Osmund Saddler, Los Illuminados and the events of Resident Evil 4.
ARCHIVE NOTE
Las Plagas is a parasite, not a virus.
FILE VX-005 // CANONCRITICAL THREAT

UROBOROS

VIRAL AGENT · TRICELL PROJECT

A bioweapon developed by Albert Wesker and Tricell using Progenitor-related material. Compatibility with a host is a major factor in its outcome.

OPEN RESEARCH FILE
ORIGIN
Tricell research in Africa, tied to Wesker’s plans.
KNOWN EFFECTS
Extensive biomass mutation and absorption of organic matter in incompatible hosts.
NOTABLE ASSOCIATIONS
Albert Wesker and the Kijuju incident in Resident Evil 5.
ARCHIVE NOTE
Uroboros is not a universal enhancement; most hosts do not achieve Wesker’s intended result.
FILE VX-006 // CANONHIGH THREAT

C-VIRUS

VIRAL AGENT · FAMILY / NEO-UMBRELLA

A hybrid viral agent associated with the global bioterror incidents depicted in Resident Evil 6. Its manifestations differ between hosts and forms.

OPEN RESEARCH FILE
ORIGIN
Developed by the organization known as The Family, with links to Neo-Umbrella operations.
KNOWN EFFECTS
Varied mutation patterns, including different forms of J’avo and other infected creatures.
NOTABLE ASSOCIATIONS
Resident Evil 6 and the Lanshiang, Edonia and Tall Oaks incidents.
ARCHIVE NOTE
Do not confuse C-Virus with the similarly named T- or G-Virus agents.
FILE VX-007 // CANONCRITICAL THREAT

MUTAMYCETE / MOLD

FUNGAL ORGANISM · CONNECTED NETWORK

A fungal superorganism associated with the Baker incident and later events. It can influence hosts and preserve biological information through a connected network.

OPEN RESEARCH FILE
ORIGIN
A naturally occurring fungal organism studied and exploited in bioweapon research.
KNOWN EFFECTS
Infection, regeneration, mutation and networked influence; effects depend on exposure and subject.
NOTABLE ASSOCIATIONS
Eveline, the Baker family, Miranda and the events of Resident Evil 7 and Village.
ARCHIVE NOTE
The Mold is fungal, not a virus. “Mutamycete” and “Megamycete” refer to related lore concepts.
FILE VX-008 // CANONSEVERE THREAT

T-ABYSS

VIRAL AGENT · ABYSS PROJECT

A virus developed through research involving the T-Virus and a marine virus found in the depths of the ocean.

OPEN RESEARCH FILE
ORIGIN
Developed by the Il Veltro-linked Abyss project.
KNOWN EFFECTS
Mutation and adaptation associated with aquatic environments; infected creatures show varied forms.
NOTABLE ASSOCIATIONS
Terragrigia, the Queen Zenobia and Resident Evil Revelations.
ARCHIVE NOTE
The T-Abyss programme is distinct from the original T-Virus outbreak.
